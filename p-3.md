## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Findings

A live end-to-end browser audit was conducted on `http://localhost:5173/` using test account `audit_user@notifyiq.test` (Workspace: `Audit Workspace`). While Phase 2 successfully delivered the Google Gemini AI Suite (`gemini-3.5-flash-lite`, `gemini-embedding-001`), multi-format RAG ingestion (.pdf, .docx, .yaml, .json, .csv, .txt), Meta WABA Profile management, and Bot Agent Control Center, an exhaustive audit revealed **critical missing fundamentals** required for commercial operation and multi-tenant scaling for 100+ business users:

1. **Non-Functional Payment Gateways**: Clicking `Checkout with Stripe` or `Checkout with Razorpay` on `/billing` performs **no action**. Stripe Checkout Sessions, Razorpay Payment Modals, and Webhook listeners are incomplete.
2. **Missing Contact Management Suite**: `/contacts` provides no interface to manually add single contacts, bulk-import CSV/Excel contact lists, filter by audience tags, or export contact records.
3. **Absence of Executive Analytics Dashboard**: Navigating to `/` lands directly in the Inbox. There is no executive `/dashboard` overview displaying delivery SLA, AI bot containment rates, or reseller profit margins.
4. **Missing Campaign Scheduling & Audience Segmentation**: `/campaigns` only supports immediate global broadcasts. Scheduled future broadcasts and tag-based audience filtering are absent.
5. **Basic WhatsApp Template Builder**: `/campaigns/templates` lacks support for interactive CTA buttons (URLs, Quick Replies, Phone Numbers) and media headers (Images, PDFs).
6. **Inbox Agent Controls**: Missing an explicit manual Bot vs. Human Agent handoff toggle inside the active conversation view.
